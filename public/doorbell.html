    How do I enroll a new tag?

    With card access disabled, send RFID ENROLL <UID> through the ESP’s USB Serial Monitor at 115200. Hold the matching tag still until RESULT=READY, then remove it. Enable access only after enrollment succeeds. This app manages names and permissions; it never reads the tag’s private keys.

      Connection and audio troubleshooting

      If the app connects but the ESP is offline, check power, Wi-Fi and its Serial Monitor. Keep the doorbell near a strong 2.4 GHz signal.
